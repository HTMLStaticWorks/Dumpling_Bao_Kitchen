The Art of the Fold

Our dumpling wrappers are made daily using only flour, water, and time. It's a delicate balance of hydration and resting that yields the perfect chew, strong enough to hold our rich, savory broths and fillings.

Each dumpling is pleated by hand—a rhythmic, meditative process that ensures a perfect seal and a beautiful presentation in the steamer basket.

Behind the Steam: The Art of the 18-Pleat Seal

Every morning at dawn, our master folding cooks begin with stone-ground heritage flour and 8-hour simmered broth. No machines, no shortcuts — just generations of craft, precision, and passion inside every cedar bamboo steamer.

✓
Heritage Unbleached Flour Hydrated & rested 4 hours for delicate translucency and tender chew.
✓
18-Fold Precision Seal Each dumpling is folded in under 12 seconds to trap scalding broth securely.
✓
8-Hour Slow Broth Aspic Gelatinized rich broth infused with young ginger and Shaoxing rice wine.
✓
Cedar Bamboo Steaming Woven baskets absorb condensation so skins stay silky, never waterlogged.
