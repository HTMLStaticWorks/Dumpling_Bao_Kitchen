Handmade Authenticity

Our Food Philosophy: The Steam Kitchen Way

Bamboo & Flour began with a simple conviction: food made with pure intent, patience, and skilled hands carries a flavor that machines can never replicate. In our kitchen, every dumpling is rolled thin at the edges, thick at the center, and sealed with traditional rhythmic folds.

We believe that food should comfort and energize. That’s why we refuse artificial binders, excess sodium, or frozen shortcuts. Every morning, our chefs begin before sunrise: simmering rich chicken and pork bone broths for eight hours, mincing crisp aromatics, and resting the flour dough to achieve that quintessential bite.

Our Distinction

What Makes Us Special

Discover the subtle nuances and painstaking techniques that make every meal at Bamboo & Flour uniquely memorable.

🤲

Master Hand-Rolling

We roll our wrappers by hand so the perimeter remains delicate and thin while the center retains strength to cradle steaming broth.

🎋

Natural Bamboo Steaming

Bamboo absorbs excess vapor to prevent sogginess, imbuing every dumpling and bao with a subtle herbal warmth.

🌶️

Artisanal House Sauces

From 24-hour slow-infused garlic chili crisp to aged Zhenjiang black vinegar, our condiments are blended in-house.

☁️

Double-Fermented Bao

Our bao dough undergoes slow dual-stage natural proofing, creating signature cloud-like texture and sweet aroma.

Uncompromising Quality

Our Ingredients & Sourcing

Great food starts with great ingredients. We partner directly with regional farms and trusted purveyors.

🥬

Fresh Vegetables

Crisp cabbage, vibrant garlic chives, and earthy shiitake mushrooms, delivered daily from local green markets and chopped every morning to maintain vital crunch and natural sweet juices.

🥩

Quality Heritage Proteins

We source pasture-raised heritage pork and free-range chicken from cooperative family farms, ensuring ethical stewardship, rich unadulterated flavor, and melt-in-mouth filling texture.

🧄

Careful Aromatics

Aged yellow ginger root, pungent scallions, stone-pressed toasted sesame oil, and artisanal Shaoxing cooking wine form the delicate aromatic backbone of our generational recipes.
